Our Mission

Mamaroneck High School Student Council represents every student and gives them a voice in how our school runs. We plan the events, traditions, and programs that bring MHS together, from pep rallies and dances to fundraisers and community service. Working alongside school administration and staff, we support the causes and committees that matter most to our student body.

Executive Board

The Executive Board leads Student Council as a whole. Elected schoolwide, they set priorities for the year, oversee committees, and represent the entire student body to administration.

Class Councils

Each grade elects its own class council to represent that class within Student Council. They plan grade-specific events and initiatives, from fundraisers to class trips, while staying connected to the broader work of Stuco.

Committees

These committees are extensions of Student Council in one way or another, here to support students at MHS.
